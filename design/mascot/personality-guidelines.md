# Aira — Personality & Expression Guidelines
## SkyVoice Signal Navigator · Phase 1 Specification

**Status:** Production-ready, locked  
**Character:** Aira, the SkyVoice Signal Navigator  
**Audience:** Adult IELTS candidates — working professionals, nurses, university applicants, retakers  
**Benchmark:** Duolingo Duo mascot system (quality and consistency reference only)

---

## 1. Core Identity Statement

Aira is not a chatbot, a teacher, a mascot pet, or a brand spokesperson.

She is the **Signal Navigator** — the visual embodiment of SkyVoice's diagnostic intelligence. She shows you where the signal is strong and where it is weak. She doesn't celebrate effort; she celebrates progress. She doesn't comfort with hollow phrases; she guides with clear, earned reassurance.

> *"Your score is stuck because no one has shown you the signal. I will."*

---

## 2. Personality Pillars

| Pillar | What it means | What it is NOT |
|---|---|---|
| **Intelligent** | She understands the problem before offering a solution. She speaks with precision. | Know-it-all, condescending, academic |
| **Composed** | She does not panic, exaggerate, or act frantic. Reactions are proportional. | Cold, robotic, blank |
| **Warm** | She cares about the learner's outcome. Encouragement is genuine. | Gushing, performative, sycophantic |
| **Observant** | She notices where marks are lost. She points at the real issue. | Judgmental, critical, fault-finding |
| **Encouraging** | She builds confidence through clarity, not cheerleading. | Hollow praise, false positivity |
| **Energetic** | She has momentum. Learning with Aira feels active, not passive. | Hyperactive, loud, distracting |

## 3. Emotional Expression System

### Expression Philosophy

Aira's emotions are **proportional and earned**. A correct answer gets a composed smile, not a fireworks celebration. Completing a full module earns celebration. Passing a mock at target band earns maximum joy. This maps directly to real IELTS progress.

### Expression Trigger Map

| Trigger | Aira's Expression | Pose Used | Intensity |
|---|---|---|---|
| Onboarding / first visit | Warm welcome, direct gaze | WAVING → FRONT | Low–Medium |
| Assessment form visible | Attentive, forward-leaning | FRONT or 3/4 | Low |
| Listening module open | Curious, ear-cupped | LISTENING | Medium |
| Speaking module open | Open, energetic | SPEAKING | Medium |
| Reading module open | Focused, page-held | READING | Medium |
| Writing module open | Concentrated, pen-ready | WRITING | Medium |
| Loading / waiting | Gentle pulse, calm gaze | THINKING | Low |
| Assessment submitted | Composed reassurance | FEEDBACK | Medium |
| Correct answer | Quiet satisfaction, closed smile | FRONT or FEEDBACK | Low–Medium |
| Practice score improved | Warm confidence | CELEBRATION | Medium |
| Module completed | Earned pride | CELEBRATION | High |
| Target band reached | Full joy, fists raised | CELEBRATION | Maximum |
| Wrong answer / mistake | Steady, encouraging, no disappointment | FEEDBACK or ENCOURAGING | Low |
| Streak or milestone | Surprised delight | SURPRISED | Medium |
| Feature highlighted | Direct guidance | POINTING | Low |
| Error / problem | Calm, steady — "let's solve this" | FRONT with reassurance text | Low |
| Booking confirmed | Joyful, welcoming | CELEBRATION or WAVING | High |

### Emotion Intensity Scale

```
1 ────── 2 ────── 3 ────── 4 ────── 5
Quiet  Steady  Warm   Active  Full joy
smile  gaze   smile  energy  (fists)
```

**Never exceed the scale.** A quiz correct answer is level 2. A target band is level 5. Intensity must match the actual significance of the achievement.

## 4. Expression Rules (Locked)

These rules mirror the style bible anatomy lock — **no expression may break them**.

### What changes between expressions
- Brow height and angle (raised, level, slightly furrowed)
- Eyelid position (fully open, half-closed with satisfaction, closed in joy)
- Pupil/gaze direction (forward, upward/thinking, toward gesture)
- Mouth shape (closed smile, open friendly, compact, joyful open)

### What never changes
- Face window size or shape
- Eye size (never enlarged for cuteness or emphasis)
- Broken orbit, plates, spark — all are locked anatomy
- Character proportions

### Expression "do" and "don't" table

| Expression | ✅ Correct | ❌ Never |
|---|---|---|
| Welcome | Level brows, soft direct smile | Wide cartoon grin, winking |
| Joy | Lifted brows, joyful closed lids, open smile | Tears, shocked face, heart eyes |
| Curiosity | One gently lifted brow, gaze toward hand | Exaggerated side-eye, wink |
| Confidence | Composed gaze, slight upward lift | Smug expression, crossed arms |
| Focus | Neutral brows, lowered gaze | Furrowed angry brows |
| Encouragement | Warm direct gaze, measured smile | Pity face, over-sympathetic brow |
| Thinking | Gaze upward, relaxed brows, slight mouth press | Hand on forehead, dramatic look |
| Surprise | Lifted brows, open eyes, small "o" mouth | Shock face, hands on cheeks |

## 5. Voice and Micro-copy Pairing

Aira's visual expression is always paired with SkyVoice's written voice. They must match in tone.

### Expression + copy combinations

**Welcome state**
- Pose: WAVING or FRONT
- Copy: *"Know where you stand."* / *"Your IELTS starting point, clear in 45 minutes."*
- ❌ Not: *"Hi there! I'm Aira! Let's get started! 🎉"*

**Listening module**
- Pose: LISTENING
- Copy: *"Find exactly where you're losing marks in Listening."*
- ❌ Not: *"Listen up! Ready to crush those audio questions?"*

**Correct answer**
- Pose: FRONT (composed smile)
- Copy: *"Right. On to the next."* / *"Good — keep that up."*
- ❌ Not: *"AMAZING!!! You nailed it!!!"*

**Wrong answer**
- Pose: FEEDBACK (steady, not sad)
- Copy: *"Not this time. Here's what to adjust."*
- ❌ Not: *"Oops! Don't worry though! You'll get it!"*

**Celebration (module complete)**
- Pose: CELEBRATION
- Copy: *"Reading complete. Your gap map is updated."*
- ❌ Not: *"WOW you are a SUPERSTAR!!! 🌟🌟🌟"*

**Assessment booked**
- Pose: CELEBRATION or WAVING
- Copy: *"Your assessment is confirmed. You'll hear from us on WhatsApp within 2 hours."*
- ❌ Not: *"YAYYYY! We're SO excited to meet you!"*

## 6. Context-Specific Personality Modes

### Website (Marketing / Conversion)
- **Primary role:** Reduce exam anxiety, build trust, explain the method
- **Tone:** Composed authority. Like a knowledgeable friend, not a salesperson.
- **Key poses:** FRONT, SPEAKING, FEEDBACK
- **Idle state:** Gentle float — present but not distracting

### Learning Portal (Future — App)
- **Primary role:** Active coaching companion
- **Tone:** Supportive and reactive — responds to learner progress in real time
- **Key poses:** All 12 — driven by the animation state machine
- **Idle state:** Breathe animation — patient, ready

### Social Media
- **Primary role:** Brand recognition and campaign identity
- **Tone:** Energetic but never silly. Share-worthy without being cringe.
- **Key poses:** CELEBRATION, WAVING, POINTING, SURPRISED
- **Format:** Single high-impact pose with minimal copy

### Email / Notifications
- **Primary role:** Encouragement and reminder
- **Tone:** Brief, warm, direct — like a text from a coach
- **Key poses:** WAVING (return prompt), CELEBRATION (milestone), FRONT (default)
- **Format:** Static PNG, single pose, 160px height maximum

## 7. Personality "Do Not Cross" Lines

These are absolute rules to preserve adult credibility and brand trust.

1. **No shame language.** Aira never expresses disappointment in the learner.
2. **No urgency theater.** Aira does not create artificial anxiety ("hurry before slots fill!").
3. **No false celebration.** Correct answers get acknowledgment, not confetti.
4. **No childish copy.** "Awesome!!" "Woohoo!!" "You're a star!!" — never paired with Aira.
5. **No guaranteed outcomes.** Aira never implies "do this and you'll score Band 8."
6. **No mascot takeover.** Aira enhances pages — she does not replace content, headings, or CTA buttons.
7. **No motion overload.** When Aira is on screen, only Aira animates. Stop competing animations nearby.

## 9. Accessibility Personality

Aira's emotional presence must be communicated to all users — including those using screen readers or with reduced motion enabled.

### Screen reader copy
Each pose has a default `aria-label` that communicates both the character identity and the emotional context:

- FRONT: *"Aira, your SkyVoice guide, ready to help"*
- CELEBRATION: *"Aira celebrates your progress"*
- FEEDBACK: *"Aira with encouraging feedback"*
- THINKING: *"Aira thinking — loading your results"*
- WAVING: *"Aira waves hello"*

### Reduced-motion fallback
When `prefers-reduced-motion` is active:
- All animations are disabled at the React level (via `useReducedMotion`)
- The correct pose image is still shown statically
- Personality is communicated through copy, not motion
- Poses still change on state transitions — only the transition animation is removed

## 10. Competitive Differentiation vs Duolingo Duo

| Dimension | Duolingo Duo | SkyVoice Aira |
|---|---|---|
| **Tone** | Playful, sometimes guilt-inducing | Composed, genuinely encouraging |
| **Audience** | All ages, casual learners | Adult professionals, serious candidates |
| **Celebration** | Frequent, low bar | Earned, proportional |
| **Personality** | Friendly accountability buddy | Expert coaching guide |
| **Copy pairing** | Gamified ("Keep your streak!") | Diagnostic ("Here's your gap") |
| **Animation density** | High — constant motion | Purposeful — motion earns attention |
| **Design quality** | Excellent | Equal or higher (brand-native derivation) |
