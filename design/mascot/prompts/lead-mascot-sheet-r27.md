# Aira Lead Mascot Sheet — Round 27 Signal Myna Pivot

Use case: original reference-guided character-system generation

## Goal

Replace the repeatedly failing human silhouette with an original premium signal myna: a compact mature common-myna-inspired speech mimic, visibly Sky Voice through a navy speech-bubble torso, cobalt three-lobe waveform wing, two-peak waveform tail, four cream module tiles, orange beak/orbital slash, and orange spark.

## Construction

- Head: sleek forward-angled navy teardrop/capsule; tiny cream side-set almond eye; restrained orange orbital slash; long slim orange wedge beak; no face mask or owl disk.
- Torso: compact upright navy speech bubble, approximately 1.45 head lengths, with one lower-right notch and exactly four cream square tiles.
- Wing: one mass of exactly three broad connected cobalt waveform lobes; same near/far rig under perspective.
- Tail: one mass of exactly two joined pointed waveform peaks, navy base and cobalt inner face.
- Legs/feet: equal medium-brown tapered legs, each ending in one connected angular wedge foot with one forward and one rear point.
- Spark: one stable orange four-point landmark above/behind the crown.

Standing proportion is approximately 3.7 head lengths. Head remains below 28% of height; beak is about 45% of head width; eyes remain below 8% of head width. Shape counts and pivots never change.

## Actions

- FRONT: balanced upright, near wing open low, head slightly toward learner.
- 3/4: coordinated walking turn and counter-swing.
- LISTENING: deep lean, head cocked, wing cupped behind side of head.
- SPEAKING: chest lifted, open beak, projecting wing, three sound bars.
- READING: low perch curled over one passage card held by folded wing tips.
- WRITING: stable crouch, one leg braced, one wing tip marks card.
- FEEDBACK: grounded lateral stance, both wings present the result card.
- CELEBRATION: upward hop, feet off ground, near wing high, far wing back, tail counterbalancing.

## Originality and finish

No owl anatomy, circular face disk, wide front-facing eyes, feather mask, ear tufts, Duo silhouette, detached circular feet, green, copied poses, pear body, fan wings, feather clutter, baby/chibi ratio, human clothing, or sports-mascot styling. Use only the locked Sky Voice six-color palette, large economical flat masses, and confident navy outlines on transparency.
