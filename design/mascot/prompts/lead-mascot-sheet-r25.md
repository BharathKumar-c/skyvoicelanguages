# Aira Lead Mascot Sheet — Round 25 Living Signal Shell

Use case: reference-guided character-system rebuild

## Inputs

1. Round 24 master, preserved as `design/mascot/rounds/lead-mascot-sheet-r24-pre-r25-preserved.png`, supplies the approved adult tone, palette, head apparatus, composition, and eight IELTS states.
2. `SKYVOICE - LOGO FILES/PNG/SKYVOICE ICON@300x-8.png` supplies the broken orbit, waveform, orange lower-left arc, and spark language.

## Single combined gap

Round 24 remained too close to stock edtech clip art: a generic humanoid body carried small identity decorations, the task poses felt mechanically stiff, and most emotions changed only through minor eyebrow/mouth edits.

## Canonical correction: living signal shell

- Aira is not a human wearing a normal vest. Her torso is one broad tapered navy speech-signal shell with rounded shoulders and a compact waist.
- The right outer flank expands into three large rounded cobalt waveform pulses followed by one pointed speech-tail. This contour occupies approximately 35% of torso width and is readable in solid silhouette at 96 px.
- The three pulses and point are one registered torso mass, not a badge, sash, cape, armor plate, skirt, loose garment, or removable prop.
- A cobalt vertical signal channel carries exactly four cream square module tiles. One small cream V-neck remains.
- White sleeve capsules, navy trouser legs, cobalt wedge shoes, and medium-brown mitten hands attach to fixed joint locations.

## Preserved head identity

- Separate cobalt upper-left and right/lower arcs retain clear top and lower-left air gaps.
- One short orange lower-left arc and one external orange four-point spark remain.
- A navy waveform crest rises above the cream face; a pointed navy waveform neck enters the shell.
- The compact cream face retains mature almond eyes, brows, minimal nose, and restrained mouth.

## Articulation and acting

- `FRONT`: confident weight shift, low open welcome hand; lifted brow and asymmetric half-smile.
- `3/4`: decisive stride with counter-swinging arms and coordinated head/shell/pelvis turn; alert lifted brow.
- `LISTENING`: deep ankle/hip lean and firmly cupped ear; narrowed eyes, high questioning brow, pursed mouth.
- `SPEAKING`: planted wide stance, one hand on diaphragm and one projecting arm; alert eyes and open orange mouth.
- `READING`: seated curl toward the passage card held in both hands; lowered lids and quiet mouth.
- `WRITING`: braced half-kneel, forward-pitched torso, working hand directed to the card; inward brows and firm mouth.
- `FEEDBACK`: low lateral presenting lunge with both arms supporting the result card; empathetic brows and warm side smile.
- `CELEBRATION`: extended rising diagonal with one arm high and one swept back; joyful crescent eyes and broad open orange smile.

All states reuse one upper-arm width, narrower forearm width, one mitten mass with a thumb notch, one thigh/calf relationship, one wedge-foot scale, and coherent shoulder/elbow/wrist/hip/knee/ankle attachments.

## Production rules

- Exact palette: cobalt `#1655D8`, navy `#0E2D5E`, orange `#EB9D13`, cream `#FFF4D6`, white `#FFFFFF`, medium-brown `#B56A45`.
- Flat 2D fills, smooth rounded navy outline, transparent background.
- No gradient, lighting, shadow, glow, haze, texture, 3D, watermark, owl/bird anatomy, green, copied pose, stock presenter styling, superhero language, or extra props.
- Generated label pills and feedback title zone stay blank for deterministic typography.
