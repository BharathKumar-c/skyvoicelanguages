# Round 28 — Aira the Signal Core

Create a premium flat 2D 4 × 2 character sheet for an original non-human, non-animal IELTS coach. The character's complete floating body is the exact Sky Voice broken-orbit voice mark: cobalt upper-left and right/lower arcs, a short orange lower-left segment, clear negative-space gaps, navy internal voice path, a warm-cream soft-shield face window, a short pointed lower speech-tail, and one orange four-point spark above-right.

Add exactly two constant-width waveform-ribbon limbs, one emerging from each open orbit end. Each ribbon has one or two broad controlled bends and the same rounded tip. Never add hands, fingers, wings, feathers, legs, shoes, clothing, a humanoid torso, animal anatomy, robot chassis, or detached feet. The mark is the body.

Lock core diameter, arc thickness, face-window ratio, ribbon width, tip radius, tail, and spark across all poses. Use small almond eyes, precise brows, a minimal nose, and restrained adult mouth shapes. FRONT must be genuinely frontal and circular; 3/4 must compress to an oblique ellipse with near/far overlap and partial far-ribbon occlusion.

Eight cells: FRONT, 3/4, LISTENING, SPEAKING, READING, WRITING, FEEDBACK, CELEBRATION. Make actions read through core tilt and ribbon mechanics. Use at most one minimal prop per task: one card for reading/writing/feedback; three signal bars for listening/speaking; none for celebration. Leave label pills and feedback typography clean for deterministic overlay.

Use only navy `#0E2D5E`, cobalt `#1655D8`, orange `#EB9D13`, cream `#FFF4D6`, and white. Transparent 1536 × 1024 canvas. Smooth editorial curves, flat color, balanced negative space. No gradient, glow, haze, texture, shadow, background, green, owl/bird anatomy, Duo silhouette, or copied poses.

Production output is cleaned by `design/mascot/process_r28.py`, which overlays typography, quantizes the palette, constructs the final alpha matte, and exports white, black, checker, silhouette, and 96 px QA proofs.
