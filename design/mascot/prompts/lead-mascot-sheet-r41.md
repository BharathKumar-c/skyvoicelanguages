# Round 41 — Signal Navigator Production Record

## Generation provenance

The white-background concept sheet was created with OpenAI's built-in image-generation capability and saved as `design/mascot/rounds/lead-mascot-sheet-r41-imagegen-white.png`. The source is the authoritative generated artwork for this round. No external stock artwork or proprietary Duolingo asset is included.

## Production intent

Preserve the image-generated Signal Navigator exactly while preparing a transparent, labeled production sheet. Remove only the uniform white matte, proportionally fit each unchanged pose into the established 4 × 2 grid, and add deterministic label chips. Do not posterize, vectorize, redraw, repaint, sharpen, or add new anatomy.

## Locked identity

- The broken Sky Voice orbit glyph is the entire head, not hair, a helmet, or a halo.
- A warm cream circular face window sits inside the orbit and keeps restrained adult proportions.
- The orbit always consists of the same navy lower-left plate, cobalt upper/outer plate, orange upper-right segment, narrow open white breaks, and orange four-point signal spark.
- The tall tapered navy/cobalt pen-nib voice core has no legs and ends in one floating navy base dot.
- Two clean articulated arms use the same navy/cobalt upper-arm, forearm, orange joint-ring, and simplified four-finger hand construction.
- One cobalt same-side speech-tail fin exits the character's left hip and points outward.
- Four cream torso module tiles remain vertically aligned in ordinary states; pose-specific overlap may hide only the tiles physically covered by a prop or arm.
- Palette is restricted to Sky Voice navy, cobalt, orange, cream, white, and warm facial brown.

## Eight-state acting library

1. `FRONT`: calm direct welcome, symmetrical arms.
2. `3/4`: confident turn, relaxed hands, stable rig.
3. `LISTENING`: curious side gaze, one hand cupping the ear aperture.
4. `SPEAKING`: open friendly mouth, two presenting hands, short orange voice rays.
5. `READING`: focused lowered gaze, cream page held clearly below the face.
6. `WRITING`: concentrated gaze, orange pen on a cream writing surface.
7. `FEEDBACK`: reassuring closed-eye smile, pointing to a large cream result card bearing only brand-native progress shapes.
8. `CELEBRATION`: joyful closed-eye expression, both fists raised, core lifted into a dynamic curve.

Expression changes are limited to brows, lids, pupils, mouth, head angle, arm articulation, and core lean. The head segments, face window, spark, torso construction, limb kit, tail fin, modules, and floating base never redesign between poses.
