# Round 38 — Deterministic Flat Aira Component System

Rebuild Aira the Signal-Starling as a precise, reusable flat-vector production rig. Round 37 is reference only for mature proportions, alert posture, and adult attitude; do not retain its gradients, feather rendering, highlight modeling, or pose-specific anatomy.

## Canonical shape kit

- One sleek navy teardrop torso.
- One long neck/head mass with a two-sweep crest and long precise signal beak.
- One cream throat wedge.
- One small adult orange eye with restrained brow and closed/open beak states.
- One cobalt wing group with exactly three broad waveform lobes and fixed internal separators.
- One tail group with exactly two broad waveform peaks and a speech-shaped notch.
- One reusable connected leg/three-toe-foot component; both legs remain connected to the lower torso.
- One orange four-point chest spark.
- One thick broken-orbit collar centered behind the upper torso. It has exactly three fixed segments—cobalt, navy, orange—with identical geometry and gaps in every state. Every segment must remain visible beyond the body contour.

All reusable geometry is stored in `design/mascot/source/lead-mascot-r38.svg`. Pose variation is created with translate, rotate, scale/mirror, head tilt, torso lean, wing pivot, tail angle, and leg stance rather than redrawing anatomy.

## Eight states

Render FRONT, true 3/4, LISTENING, SPEAKING, READING, WRITING, FEEDBACK, and CELEBRATION in a 4 × 2 transparent 1536 × 1024 sheet. The states read as welcoming, attentive, curious, confidently speaking, focused, concentrated, empathetic, and joyful. Eyes remain small and mature. Speaking and celebration alone open the beak. Props are large, cream, minimal, and secondary.

## Rendering

Premium adult editorial flat 2D. Use navy `#0E2D5E`, cobalt `#1655D8`, orange `#EB9D13`, cream `#FFF4D6`, and white `#FFFFFF` only. No gradients, glow, shadow, texture, feather clutter, thick sticker perimeter, or atmospheric pixels. Navy separator strokes appear only where they clarify wing lobes, tail rhythm, and props.

## Originality guard

No owl disk, owl or horn tufts, green, Duo silhouette, copied Duolingo pose, detached feet, giant pupils, angry V brows, parrot hook beak, penguin body, human hands, gloves, costume, or literal IELTS logo.
