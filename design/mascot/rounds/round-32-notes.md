# Round 32 Builder Notes

## Intent

Retain the Signal Navigator's owned voice/nib/orbit identity while replacing the outlined clip-art finish with a premium editorial plane system.

## Changes

- Reduced the halo to three bold broken-orbit plates with generous gaps.
- Removed thick perimeter contours from filled forms.
- Introduced subtle action-specific core curves while preserving length/width anchors.
- Removed elbow disks, cuff bands, and cartoon glove construction.
- Standardized minimal three-prong signal tips and simplified prop contact points.
- Retained warm differentiated faces and all eight learning actions.

## Deterministic finish

`design/mascot/process_r32.py` quantizes the five-color palette, overlays labels and feedback content, builds shape-colored alpha bands without polygonizing the generated curves, clears transparent RGB, and exports five QA proofs.

## QA

White, silhouette, and 96 px proofs confirm that the core, broken orbit rhythm, action curves, and arm gestures remain legible after the visual simplification.
