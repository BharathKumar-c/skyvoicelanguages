# Round 33 — Builder Notes

## Result

Aira is rebuilt as a kinetic emblem rather than a humanoid coach. The tall cream/navy voice-to-pen core remains the identity anchor, while three broken-orbit signal vanes provide the complete pose language. No conventional arm, hand, finger, shoulder, elbow, glove, wing, or grasping anatomy remains.

## State logic

- `FRONT`: canonical asymmetric broken-orbit rhythm.
- `3/4`: compressed vane grouping and narrowed core create the canonical turn.
- `LISTENING`: two vanes cup inward toward a three-bar audio mark.
- `SPEAKING`: vanes fan outward toward three orange speech bars.
- `READING`: cobalt vanes bracket a floating open page.
- `WRITING`: one vane tip meets the page as a stylus while the other vanes counterbalance.
- `FEEDBACK`: vanes frame and underline the floating `BAND 7.5` panel.
- `CELEBRATION`: all three vanes sweep upward without confetti or a handheld prop.

## Rationale

The Kinetic Emblem removes the recurring weak point from prior rounds: inconsistent human limb and hand construction. Its animation grammar is now component-based and brand-native. The same central core remains recognizable while the three orbit-derived vanes make listening, speaking, reading, writing, feedback, and celebration legible through broad directional arrangements at small sizes.
