# Round 33 Builder Notes

## Intent

Evolve the Signal Navigator into a non-anatomical Kinetic Emblem whose three Sky Voice orbit plates perform every gesture.

## Changes

- Removed all arms, hands, fingers, shoulders, elbows, gloves, cuffs, and grasping.
- Converted the two cobalt and one orange orbit plates into three pivoted fixed-size signal vanes.
- Reframed actions as vane configurations: cup, fan, bracket, trace, frame, underline, and upward sweep.
- Converted reading, writing, and feedback props into suspended interface panels.
- Preserved the elongated core, nib, spark, palette, tiny adult expression system, and eight-state structure.

## Deterministic finish

`design/mascot/process_r33.py` quantizes the five-color palette, overlays labels and feedback content, creates shape-colored antialias bands, clears transparent RGB, and exports five QA proofs.

## QA

White, silhouette, and 96 px proofs confirm that all eight configurations remain legible without limb anatomy and that the broken-orbit signal-vane identity survives reduction.
