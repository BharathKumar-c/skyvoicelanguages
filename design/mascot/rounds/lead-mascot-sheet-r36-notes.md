# Round 36 — Builder Notes

## Result

Round 36 deliberately pivots from the abstract Directional Lens to Aira the adult Signal-Starling. The supplied high-quality white-background reference was produced with the built-in image-generation skill and is treated as the exact identity source.

The eight poses retain their original scale and placement.
