## Recoverable Round 2 decisions

- Use materially different whole-body action lines rather than near-identical standing poses.
- Preserve a visibly broader emotion range, especially curiosity and surprise.
- Let celebration alter the full body, not only the arms.

These are behavioral lessons only. The wand, fantasy costume, purple palette, glow, boots, humanoid anatomy, and dark presentation must not carry forward.

## Round 43 defects to correct without redesign

1. Replace glossy tonal modeling with controlled flat solid planes and consistent navy contour logic.
2. Make 3/4 clearly distinct from FRONT through head ellipse, near/far overlap, shoulder/core rotation, and hand depth.
3. Normalize hand construction, finger count, elbow rings, and wrist scale.
4. Increase label-free action separation between READING, WRITING, and FEEDBACK.
5. Preserve every identity landmark and the restrained adult face.
