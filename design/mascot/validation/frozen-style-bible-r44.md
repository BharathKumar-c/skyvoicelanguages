# Sky Voice Mascot Style Bible

**Lead character:** Aira, the Sky Voice Signal Navigator  
**Identity:** Premium adult coaching guide built from the Sky Voice broken-orbit and speech-signal language  
**Canonical source:** `apps/web/public/mascot/lead-mascot-sheet.png` (Round 43 baseline until a passing candidate is promoted)  
**Tone:** Intelligent, composed, warm, observant, encouraging, energetic  
**Revision:** Round 44 preflight — Round 43 architecture frozen; flat production rendering restored

## 1. Identity

Aira is an original floating signal guide for serious IELTS learners. The Sky Voice broken-orbit glyph is her entire head, while a tapered pen-nib/voice core and articulated coaching hands translate language practice into action. She is not human, animal, robot, headset agent, superhero, or toy. The silhouette is calm and professional enough for adult candidates while remaining expressive and immediately brand-native.

## 2. Locked anatomy and landmarks

- The head is one large broken orbit surrounding a smaller warm-cream circular face window. The mature face remains visually subordinate to the brand glyph rather than filling the head.
- The orbit uses four permanent landmarks: a navy lower-left plate, a cobalt upper/outer plate, an orange upper-right segment, and one orange four-point spark outside that segment.
- Narrow open gaps always separate the orbit segments. They must remain visually open on light, dark, and checker backgrounds.
- The body is one tall tapered pen-nib/voice core: cobalt shoulder plane, navy center plane, and cobalt outer edge. It has no waist, hips, legs, or feet.
- The core ends in one small floating navy base dot, separated from the body by a consistent open gap.
- Two arms attach at the same shoulder positions. Each uses the same thick navy/cobalt upper arm and forearm, clean orange joint rings, and substantial simplified four-finger navy hands. Limb thickness and hand scale do not drift between poses.
- One cobalt speech-tail fin exits the character's left hip in every state and ends in one rounded point.
- Four warm-cream module tiles form one vertical torso column. They stay equal in size and spacing unless a prop or arm physically occludes them.

No pose may relocate, restyle, add, or remove the head plates, face window, spark, core planes, shoulder roots, arm/hand kit, tail root, module column, or floating base.

## 3. Adult expression library

- **Welcome:** level brows, direct gaze, small closed smile.
- **Confidence:** composed three-quarter gaze, gently lifted outer brow, small smile.
- **Listening/curiosity:** gaze toward the listening hand, one lifted brow, compact smile.
- **Speaking/clarity:** direct bright eyes and one open, friendly speaking mouth.
- **Reading/focus:** lowered gaze, relaxed brows, compact closed smile.
- **Writing/concentration:** lowered side gaze and a restrained concentrated mouth.
- **Feedback/reassurance:** relaxed closed lids and a measured smile.
- **Celebration/earned joy:** joyful closed lids, open smile, and raised fists.

Facial changes occur only through brows, lids, pupil direction, and mouth. The face window never changes size or shape, the eyes never become oversized, and the expression never becomes angry, frantic, babyish, or sarcastic.

## 4. Rig and pose rules

- The head may tilt and rotate visually, but all orbit segments and the external spark move together as one locked head assembly.
- Shoulders rotate from fixed roots. Elbows articulate at the orange rings; wrists and four-finger hands retain their construction.
- The pen-nib core may lean or curve gently, but its plane hierarchy and tapered point remain recognizable.
- The speech-tail fin stays rooted on the same left side. It may lift or trail with the action without switching sides or becoming a cape.
- The base dot floats beneath the nib point and may follow vertical action, but never attaches as a foot.
- Whole-body silhouette and arm gesture must communicate the action before props or labels are read.

## 5. Eight canonical states

| State | Pose mechanism | Character cue |
|---|---|---|
| FRONT | Upright core, symmetrical relaxed arms | Calm direct welcome |
| 3/4 | Head/core turn, relaxed hands | Attentive confidence |
| LISTENING | Core leans, one hand cups the ear aperture | Curious side gaze |
| SPEAKING | Two open presenting hands, short voice rays | Friendly open speech |
| READING | Page held below the face, lowered gaze | Thoughtful focus |
| WRITING | One hand supports the surface, one writes | Quiet concentration |
| FEEDBACK | One hand holds the result card, one points | Reassuring evaluation |
| CELEBRATION | Core lifts and curves, both fists rise | Earned joy |

## 6. Props

- READING uses one large warm-cream page with no tiny copy.
- WRITING uses one warm-cream writing surface and one restrained orange pen.
- FEEDBACK uses one warm-cream card with a simple Sky Voice orbit/progress mark; it does not imitate an official IELTS score report.
- SPEAKING may use up to three short orange voice rays.
- Props remain secondary and never cover the face, orange spark, shoulder roots, tail root, or entire module column.
- No headset, wand, graduation cap, clock, literal IELTS logo, laptop, microphone, or customer-support device.

## 7. Palette and rendering

| Color | Hex | Role |
|---|---:|---|
| Sky Voice navy | `#0E2D5E` | Deep orbit plate, core plane, hands, base dot |
| Sky Voice cobalt | `#1655D8` | Main orbit plate, core edge, arms, tail fin |
| Sky Voice orange | `#EB9D13` | Orbit segment, signal spark, joint rings, action accents |
| Signal cream | `#FFF4D6` | Face window, torso modules, paper and result card |
| Warm brown family | source-rendered | Eyes, brows, mouth |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Open negative space and label chips |

- Preserve the Round 43 Signal Navigator architecture and landmarks, not its glossy pixel rendering.
- Render rounded flat 2D artwork with consistent confident navy contour lines and deliberately separated solid planes.
- Use no gradient, glossy highlight, soft volumetric shading, posterization band, sticker rim, or aggressive sharpening. Volume comes only from the locked navy/cobalt/orange planes and clean overlap.
- No atmospheric background, cast shadow, glow, haze, or texture overlay.
- Transparent extraction must preserve cream foreground forms while keeping the orbit gaps open and edges free of white matte.

## 8. Typography and labels

- Canonical labels: `FRONT`, `3/4`, `LISTENING`, `SPEAKING`, `READING`, `WRITING`, `FEEDBACK`, `CELEBRATION`.
- Labels use a bold installed sans-serif fallback, uppercase navy on white rounded chips with a navy edge and restrained cobalt inner keyline.
- Labels are deterministic production overlays, never generated lettering.

## 9. Originality guard

- No owl or bird anatomy, green palette, Duo silhouette, detached circular feet, copied Duolingo pose, or proprietary Duolingo shape.
- No conventional human coach, headset agent, superhero, fantasy familiar, pen character, or generic corporate robot.
- The full broken-orbit head, external orange spark, tapered floating voice core, same-side speech-tail fin, cream module column, and articulated coaching hands must survive every state and small-size use.
