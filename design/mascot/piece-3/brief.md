# Piece 3 — Website Artwork + Integration

## Round 1 goal

Integrate only the validated mascot artwork into the live SkyVoice website, with
Aira leading the free IELTS assessment experience and the Round 4 Skill Signals
supporting the four IELTS modules.

## Acceptance checks

- Aira appears prominently in the assessment hero and in a relevant completion
  context without changing the validated Piece 1 Round 44 artwork.
- Listening, Reading, Writing, and Speaking cards each use their validated Piece
  2 Round 4 active Skill Signal.
- Skill Signals remain subordinate to Aira, use fixed registration, and expose
  correct decorative semantics when adjacent headings already name each module.
- Desktop and 390 px mobile layouts remain coherent with no horizontal overflow.
- All mascot motion respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- No legacy mascot drawing or substitute icon appears in the integrated areas.
