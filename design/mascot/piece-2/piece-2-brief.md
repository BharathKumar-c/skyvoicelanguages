# Piece 2 Brief — Aira Skill Signals

**Deliverable:** four compact supporting companions plus a reusable pose/state system.

## Role hierarchy

- Aira remains the only lead mascot, the only full character, and the only figure with a face, articulated arms, hands, and adult coaching expressions.
- The supporting cast is a set of four small animated **Skill Signals** released from Aira's permanent four-module torso rail.
- Each signal is a faceless, limbless learning-status object, never a rival mascot.
- At normal use, a Skill Signal is 28–36% of Aira's head diameter; it may enlarge only in a dedicated skill card.

## Cast

| Signal | IELTS role | Locked visual mechanism |
|---|---|---|
| Echo | Listening | circular cream receiver inside a broken navy/cobalt orbit, with three restrained orange sound ticks |
| Leaf | Reading | cream page tile with a cobalt folded corner, navy reading notch, and one orange locator tab |
| Nib | Writing | cream module tile with a navy diagonal nib/slash, cobalt trailing stroke, and one orange ink dot |
| Vox | Speaking | cream speech capsule inside a broken navy/cobalt signal ring, with two orange voice ticks |

## Shared state grammar

1. **Idle:** upright, compact, no action marks; indicates availability.
2. **Active:** a small directional lean plus skill-specific orange action marks; indicates the learner's current task.
3. **Success:** a modest upward lift with the external four-point orange spark; indicates completion without imitating Aira's full celebration pose.

All states preserve the same core silhouette, outer plate count, cream center, and accent placement. Motion implication comes from lean, spacing, and no more than three orange action marks.

## Production constraints

- Exact palette: navy `#0E2D5E`, cobalt `#1655D8`, orange `#EB9D13`, cream `#FFF4D6`, white only for proof backgrounds.
- Flat 2D solid planes, confident navy contour, open negative-space gaps, no gradients, gloss, glow, cast shadow, texture, or atmospheric backdrop.
- No faces, eyes, mouths, hands, arms, legs, wings, feathers, animal anatomy, humanoid body, headset, microphone, graduation cap, score-report imitation, or copied Duolingo silhouette.
- Production output must be transparent and remain identifiable at 48 px; proof sheet must include 96 px and 48 px checks.
- Generated lettering is forbidden. All labels are deterministic overlays.

## Acceptance checks

- Four roles can be distinguished by silhouette before reading labels.
- The three states read consistently across all four companions.
- Every signal visibly derives from Aira's cream module rail and broken-orbit/spark language.
- Aira remains visually dominant when shown beside all four signals.
- Colors, contour behavior, transparency, and small-size output follow the validated Round 44 system.
