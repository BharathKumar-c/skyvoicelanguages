# Skill Signals — Round 1 Generation Prompt

Use case: illustration-story

Asset type: transparent production character-system sheet for a premium adult IELTS learning platform.

Input image: the approved Round 44 Aira sheet is a strict visual-language and palette reference only. Do not redraw Aira and do not copy a pose from it.

Primary request: create four tiny original faceless, limbless animated Skill Signal companions derived from the four warm-cream module tiles on Aira's torso. Arrange a clean four-column by three-row sheet. Columns are Listening, Reading, Writing, and Speaking. Rows are Idle, Active, and Success. Show only the twelve signal objects with generous separation and no labels or text.

Locked companion designs:

- Listening: circular cream receiver inside a broken navy/cobalt orbit, plus three restrained orange sound ticks only in Active.
- Reading: cream page tile with a cobalt folded corner, a navy reading notch, and one orange locator tab.
- Writing: cream module tile with a navy diagonal nib/slash, a cobalt trailing stroke, and one orange ink dot.
- Speaking: cream speech capsule inside a broken navy/cobalt signal ring, plus two orange voice ticks only in Active.

State grammar:

- Idle is upright and compact with no action marks.
- Active leans gently toward its action and uses at most three orange action marks.
- Success lifts modestly and gains the same external four-point orange spark used by the approved Aira system.
- Preserve each signal's core silhouette, plate count, cream center, and accent placement across all three states.

Style: flat rounded 2D illustration, solid color planes, confident consistent dark-navy outer contour, deliberately open negative-space gaps, adult and precise rather than childish.

Palette: use only `#0E2D5E` dark navy, `#1655D8` cobalt blue, `#EB9D13` warm orange, and `#FFF4D6` signal cream. Transparent background.

Hierarchy: these are small learning-status objects, not full mascots. No faces, eyes, mouths, brows, bodies, arms, hands, legs, feet, wings, feathers, animal anatomy, or humanoid anatomy.

Avoid: owl or bird cues, green, copied Duolingo silhouette or poses, rival full characters, robot or toy styling, headset, microphone, graduation cap, text, letters, numbers, logos, labels, gradients, glossy highlights, bevels, glow, cast shadows, atmospheric backgrounds, texture, sticker rims, or watermark.
