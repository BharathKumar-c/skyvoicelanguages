# Skill Signals — Round 2 Generation Prompt

Use case: illustration-story

Asset type: transparent production character-system sheet for a premium adult IELTS learning platform.

Input image: the frozen Round 1 Skill Signals sheet. Preserve the four roles, flat Sky Voice palette, adult tone, faceless/limbless hierarchy, transparent presentation, and four-column by three-row organization. Rebuild the family architecture and state motion described below.

Primary request: make all four companions unmistakably one living Aira-derived signal family rather than four separate app icons. Every role must use the same shared broken-orbit/module carrier: three thick rounded outer plates in dark navy and cobalt, three clean open gaps, and one centered warm-cream skill module. Listening, Reading, Writing, and Speaking differ only through the silhouette/glyph of the warm-cream center module.

Locked role centers:

- Listening: a circular cream receiver with one deep navy inward listening notch; no speech tail.
- Reading: a compact cream open-page/book center with one cobalt page fold and one navy center crease.
- Writing: a compact cream rounded-square center with one bold diagonal navy nib and one short cobalt writing stroke.
- Speaking: a cream speech capsule with a long unmistakable downward speech tail and one small orange voice dot; no listening notch.

Shared state mechanics must transform the carrier itself:

- Idle: carrier plates sit close and evenly compressed around the center; three gaps are narrow and balanced; object is upright and grounded.
- Active: carrier expands about fifteen percent; its three gaps visibly widen and rotate toward the action direction; the role center shifts slightly toward that opening. Up to two short orange action ticks may reinforce the motion, but the expansion and directional gaps must communicate Active without them.
- Success: carrier lifts and opens upward like a released signal; the upper gap becomes dramatically larger, the two upper plates spread apart, and the four-point orange spark follows a short rising trajectory of two decreasing orange dots. Success must not be the Idle pose with a spark pasted beside it.

Consistency: identical outer carrier plate count, contour thickness, gap logic, and overall scale in all four columns. Keep rounded contours mechanically regular, with no wobbly or melted edges. Preserve each role center across the three states.

Small-size priority: Listening and Speaking must remain clearly different at 48 px. Listening reads as a round receiver with an inward notch. Speaking reads as a speech capsule with a long downward tail and orange voice dot.

Style: flat rounded 2D illustration, solid color planes, consistent confident dark-navy outer contour, deliberately open negative-space gaps, adult and precise rather than childish.

Palette: use only `#0E2D5E` dark navy, `#1655D8` cobalt blue, `#EB9D13` warm orange, and `#FFF4D6` signal cream. Genuinely transparent background.

Hierarchy: tiny learning-status objects, never full mascots. No faces, eyes, mouths, brows, bodies, arms, hands, legs, feet, wings, feathers, animal anatomy, or humanoid anatomy.

Avoid: four unrelated icon containers; plain document or app icons outside the shared orbit; identical Listening and Speaking centers; state change made only by added rays, sparkle, or tiny rotation; owl or bird cues; green; copied Duolingo silhouette or poses; robot or toy styling; headset; microphone; graduation cap; text; letters; numbers; logos; labels; gradients; glossy highlights; bevels; glow; cast shadows; texture; sticker rims; watermark.
