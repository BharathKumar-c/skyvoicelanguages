# Design Loop Bar: Original Sky Voice Mascot System

## Benchmark

- Primary rendered reference: <https://www.duolingo.com/>
- Supporting construction reference: <https://design.duolingo.com/illustration/characters>
- Supporting mascot reference: <https://design.duolingo.com/illustration/duo>
- Reviewed: 2026-10-13

The benchmark is Duolingo's level of character-system discipline, not its owl, cast, palette, proprietary shapes, or trade dress. The Sky Voice result must remain visibly original.

## Observable mechanisms

1. **A locked silhouette survives every pose.** At favicon or 48 px height, the lead character remains identifiable from its outer contour and two or three permanent landmarks. Poses may bend or squash those landmarks, but may not replace, relocate, or restyle them.

2. **Construction uses a small repeatable shape kit.** The head and torso each resolve to one or two primary geometric masses; limbs, hands, and facial features use the fewest shapes needed. A front view and three-quarter view must look assembled from the same parts, with no added detail that exists only in one angle.

3. **Emotion changes through controlled articulation, not redesign.** Happiness, excitement, sadness, curiosity, surprise, and confidence must read from eyes, mouth, posture, and one supporting gesture while the base proportions, core colors, and silhouette landmarks remain unchanged.

4. **Every marketing section has one dominant character action.** A single character or compact tableau occupies roughly 35–45% of the desktop section beside one headline, one short paragraph, and at most one primary action. The illustration and copy alternate left/right between consecutive sections, with at least one-third of the frame left as open space.

5. **Character color and interface color have different jobs.** Interface chrome stays within the approved Sky Voice white, blue, navy, and orange tokens. Character art may use a separate four-to-five-color secondary palette, but the lead mascot keeps one dominant color assignment across every asset and never borrows Duolingo green.

6. **Poses read at thumbnail size before details are visible.** Standing, sitting, walking, waving, thinking, and celebrating each have a distinct action line and limb arrangement. A pose fails if its activity or mood cannot be identified from a 96 px thumbnail without reading nearby copy.

7. **Motion preserves the silhouette and resolves gently.** Idle loops keep the torso anchored while one or two parts—eyes, head, hand, or a waveform-derived feature—carry the action. Movement uses one anticipation, one readable action, and one return to the exact opening pose over 3–6 seconds; different characters start at offset times so they never move in sync.

## Originality guard

- No owl anatomy, feather mask, Duo ear silhouette, detached circular feet, Duolingo green, or copied Duolingo poses.
- Derive the lead mascot from Sky Voice's waveform, star, listening, speaking, and score-progress identity.
- Keep the tone encouraging, expert, energetic, calm, and credible for adult IELTS learners.
